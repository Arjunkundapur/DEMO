Talking Points
Slide 1 of 7

Set the scene. This project is a full end-to-end data engineering pipeline built entirely within 24 hours as a personal challenge. It's a proof of what's possible when you combine the right tools — Snowflake, dbt Cloud, and Tableau — with a clear medallion architecture pattern.

Lead with the "24 hours" hook. It immediately signals speed, capability, and engineering discipline.

Let the numbers speak. Four business metrics delivered. Three clean architecture layers. One fully interactive Tableau dashboard. And the whole thing shipped in under 24 hours — not as a demo, but as a working, queryable production-style pipeline.

Pause after each count-up finishes. Allow the audience a moment to register each number.

Explain the medallion pattern. This is the industry standard for modern data lakehouses. Raw data lands in Bronze as views — no transformation, just ingestion. Silver applies cleaning, typing, and joins to produce reliable tables. Gold is where business logic lives: aggregations, metrics, KPIs.

This slide is conceptual. Don't rush it — make sure the audience understands why each layer exists before moving on.

Walk through the stack. Snowflake provides the TPCH_SF1 sample database — a standard benchmarking dataset used across the industry. dbt Cloud handles all SQL transformations using a code-first, version-controlled approach. Tableau connects to the Gold layer and renders the final dashboards.

If your audience is non-technical, focus on the outcome: "each tool had one job and they all fit together cleanly."

Zoom in on the layers. Bronze sources directly from Snowflake's TPCH_SF1 schema as lightweight views — no duplication. Silver materializes as tables after applying data quality rules and joins across fact and dimension tables. Gold runs the final business aggregations and is what Tableau reads from.

Point to each layer as you describe it. The color coding makes it easy to follow.

These are the KPIs the Gold layer surfaces. Monthly and yearly revenue is broken down both by nation and by individual customer. The Top 20 customers ranking uses window functions in dbt. Revenue by nation covers both customer-side and supplier-side flows. Segment analysis maps revenue to Snowflake's five market segments.

Each metric corresponds to a model in the Gold schema. Mention that these are all live, queryable tables.

Close with the output. Everything built in this pipeline surfaces in a single Tableau dashboard hosted on Tableau Cloud. The link is live — click through and explore the revenue breakdowns, customer rankings, and segment distributions directly.

If presenting live, open the Tableau link now and walk through one chart. That's the best possible demo.
