# DEMO

An end to end pipeline development with medallion level architecture built under 24 hours. I just tried this as a challenge. I have used the TPCH_SF1 database on snowflake and used dbt cloud for transformations and calculated metrics such as:

1) Monthly and yearly revenue (by nation and customer)
2) Top 20 customers
3) Revenue by nation - customer and supplier
4)Revenue by market segment
